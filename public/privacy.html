Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Cardo Board ("we", "our", or "the app") provides real-time spice market auction intelligence, price benchmarks, and climate telemetry across the Western Ghats region. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to transparency and user privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Cardo Board is engineered with privacy as a foundational principle:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require account registration, phone numbers, email addresses, names, or financial credentials.
  • Anonymous Usage Telemetry: We collect anonymous, aggregated diagnostic data (such as general device type e.g. Mobile/Desktop and active dashboard tab) to monitor server load and optimize data distribution.
  • No Device Permissions: The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, biometric sensors, GPS precise location, or internal file storage.

2. Third-Party Services

The app communicates directly with cloud infrastructure (hosted via Supabase and Vercel) strictly to query public certified auction reports and weather telemetry. No personal data is shared, sold, or rented to advertisers.

3. Children's Privacy

Cardo Board does not knowingly collect or solicit any information from anyone under the age of 13. The application is intended for general agricultural and spice commodity market informational purposes.

4. Security

All communication between Cardo Board and our backend data feeds is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the development team through the official project repository at GitHub.
